### Contact form
Use the form on `/contact` for general inquiries. You'll need to provide your full name, email, phone number, a category, a subject, and a message (minimum 20 characters). You'll receive on-screen confirmation once your message is received; our team typically follows up by phone or email.

### Emergency situations
The Contact page includes a dedicated emergency section — for urgent medical situations, please call the number provided there or your local emergency services directly; the website contact form is not monitored in real time.

## 3. Booking a Free Assessment

Visit `/appointments` (or the "Book an Assessment" call-to-action on the homepage/contact page) to request a free in-home care assessment. You'll be asked for:

- Your name, phone, and email
- The patient's name and age
- Your location
- The type of service you're interested in
- A preferred date/time (optional)
- Notes describing the care need (minimum 10 characters)

After submitting, our care coordination team reviews the request and will contact you to confirm scheduling.

## 4. Referring a Patient (Healthcare Partners)

If you're a clinician, hospital, or organization referring a patient, use the **referral form** on the Contact page. You'll need the organization name, referrer name, patient name, contact details, primary diagnosis, service needed, preferred start date (optional), location, and clinical notes (minimum 20 characters). Our operations team reviews all referrals and follows up directly.

## 5. Applying for a Job

1. Browse open roles on `/careers`.
2. Select a position and fill out the application form: full name, email, phone, the position you're applying for, your experience (minimum 10 characters), and a short motivation statement (minimum 20 characters).
3. Submit — you'll see an on-screen confirmation. Our HR team reviews applications and reaches out to shortlisted candidates.

## 6. Subscribing to the Newsletter

Enter your email (and optionally your name) in the newsletter signup found on the homepage/footer to receive updates from YourHomeCare. You can unsubscribe at any time by contacting us directly.

## 7. Reading the Blog

Visit `/blog` for articles on home care planning, caregiving tips, and company updates. Click any article to read the full post at `/blog/<slug>`.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

Visit `/faq` for answers to common questions about services, scheduling, and care plans. If your question isn't answered there, use the contact form.

## 9. Accessibility & Browser Support

The site is built with modern, responsive web standards (Next.js 16 / React 19 / Tailwind CSS 4) and is optimized for current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on both desktop and mobile devices.

## 10. Privacy Note

Information submitted through contact, assessment, referral, career, and newsletter forms is sent to the YourHomeCare team for follow-up purposes and stored securely in our systems. We do not display any personal information you submit publicly. For questions about how your data is used, contact us via the [Contact page](https://yourhomecare.co.ke/contact).
